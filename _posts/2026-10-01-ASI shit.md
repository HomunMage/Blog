# Artificial Shit Intelligence (ASI): How the AI Hype Machine Spoiled Mathematics, Engineering, and Creative Work

For the past few years, big tech conglomerates, venture capitalists, and marketing teams have paraded a grand narrative: *AI is going to revolutionize every industry, replace human labor, and solve complex global problems at the click of a button.* 

Yet, as we approach the mid-2020s, anyone working on the frontlines of real-world production—whether in mathematics, software engineering, music production, or visual arts—is coming to a stark, collective realization: 

**AI isn't leading us toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). It is flooding the world with Artificial Shit Intelligence (ASI).**

Instead of building robust, modular, and precise engineering tools, the tech industry has forced "probabilistic guessers" into environments that demand deterministic precision. The result? A massive offshore dumping ground of digital garbage, where Big Tech reaps the profits while skilled human workers are forced to act as glorified, underpaid janitors cleaning up AI-generated messes.

Here is a breakdown of how the AI hype machine is actively degrading human intelligence, engineering principles, and creative ecosystems across every major domain.

---

## 1. The Death of the Journey: How AI Ruined Mathematics

In mathematics, the value of proving a theorem has rarely been about the simple binary answer of *True* or *False*. The true power of mathematics lies in **the path taken to climb the mountain**. 

When mathematicians spent years trying to solve Fermat’s Last Theorem or the Poincaré Conjecture, they didn't just reach the peak—they invented entirely new theoretical frameworks (such as modern algebraic geometry and Ricci flow) that laid the groundwork for modern physics, cryptography, and computer science.

Enter the AI approach:
* **The Helicopter Fallacy**: Automated theorem provers and Large Language Models fly a helicopter straight to the top of the mountain and declare "Conquered!" without revealing the path. 
* **Demoralizing the Climbers**: When AI bypasses the journey or relies on brute-force, black-box verification, it destroys the incentive for young mathematicians to build new conceptual tools.
* **Human Degradation**: Instead of being creative explorers, mathematicians are increasingly relegated to "prompt-glue workers"—fixing syntax, feeding context, and patching formalization glitches for AI provers like Lean. 

When you eliminate the struggle of the climb, you eliminate the very tools that advance human knowledge.

## 2. Structural Sabotage of Engineering & Creative Workflows

Professional creative and technical work relies on three non-negotiable principles: **Modularity, Editability, and Deterministic Control**. AI violates all three.

### A. Music Production: Flattened Waveforms with Zero Control
* **The Reality of Production**: Professional audio work (DAWs) relies on separate stems (drums, bass, vocals), MIDI scores, precise automation curves, and the ability to edit a single measure.
* **The AI Garbage**: Tools like Suno or Udio output a single, baked MP3 waveform. It sounds impressive to a layman, but if a producer needs to mute the snare drum in bar 16 or change a bassline’s key, the AI is completely useless. You cannot edit a black-box audio file without destroying the whole track.

### B. Visual Arts & Design: Destructive Pixel Renders
* **The Reality of Production**: Designers require layered PSDs, vector nodes, line-art/shading separation, and precise masking.
* **The AI Garbage**: AI art generators output flattened, single-layer pixel arrays. When a client asks to "raise the character’s left arm by 5 degrees and change the palette to cold tones," the AI cannot edit its own logic—it has to regenerate the entire image from scratch, destroying all previous details and consistency.

### C. Software Engineering: Ignoring Specs and Breaking Constraints
* **The Reality of Production**: Writing software requires strict adherence to project architecture, design patterns, linting rules, and interface specifications (such as Model Context Protocol / MCP).
* **The AI Garbage**: Despite claims that larger context windows would solve instruction-following, AI models routinely ignore system constraints and post-instruction prompts. They hallucinate nonexistent APIs, rewrite working codebase modules behind your back, and introduce subtle, deeply embedded security vulnerabilities.

## 4. The Poisoning of Open-Source Culture

Historically, Open Source Software (OSS) thrived on a **tacit mentorship model**. A junior developer submitted a Pull Request (PR), a senior maintainer reviewed it, pointed out flaws, and guided the junior. Over time, the junior grew into a maintainer.

AI has completely poisoned this loop:
* **The AI PR Tsunami**: Maintainers are overwhelmed by thousands of AI-generated PRs that look clean on the surface but are filled with hidden bugs or bloated logic.
* **Zero Human Growth**: When a maintainer takes the time to leave detailed feedback, the contributor doesn't learn—they simply copy-paste the feedback back into ChatGPT and submit a new hallucinated iteration. 
* **Burnout**: Senior maintainers are abandoning open-source projects due to sheer fatigue from reviewing low-effort "AI slop," severing the generational chain of software craft.

## Conclusion: "Generation" Is Not "Engineering"

The tech industry’s fundamental error was mistaking **probabilistic text/pixel/waveform generation** for **scalable engineering**.

Generating a plausibly shaped result is trivial; building an editable, maintainable, deterministic, and modular system is where true value lies. Until AI systems can guarantee 100% strict instruction compliance, layer-based structural isolation, and full logical transparency, they will remain what they are today: **uncontrollable, high-maintenance garbage generators.**

It is time to look past the marketing demos, call out the cost-externalization scam, and demand tools that actually respect the principles of real engineering and human craftsmanship.
